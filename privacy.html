Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Base ("Base," "we," "us") is a symptom-tracking app for people managing chronic conditions. This policy explains what information we collect, how it's used, where it's stored, and the choices and rights you have over it.

The short version: Your symptom data is yours. We collect only what the app needs to work, store it securely with our processor Supabase, never sell it, and never share it with advertisers. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Symptom logs: pain, fatigue, brain fog, mood, sleep, stress, weather tags, and food-trigger tags you log each day.
  • Condition selections: the chronic condition(s) you tell us you're managing during onboarding (e.g. fibromyalgia, EDS, POTS).
  • Medications: medication names and dosages you add, and whether you logged taking them on a given day.
  • Account identifiers: if you choose to sign in, we receive a unique, anonymized identifier from Sign in with Apple, and optionally the name/email you choose to share through Apple's sign-in flow (Apple allows you to hide your real email behind a relay address).

Information we do not require

Base is fully usable as a guest, with no account. If you never sign in, your data stays only on your device and we never receive it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core functionality of the app: storing your daily logs and displaying them back to you.
  • To generate the pattern-insight features and PDF doctor reports you explicitly request.
  • If you sign in, to sync and back up your data across your devices and restore it if you get a new phone.
  • To operate account security features (rate limiting, session management, and account deletion).

We do not use your health data to train advertising models, build user profiles for marketing, or personalize ads. We have no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

3. We Do Not Sell or Share Your Data for Advertising

We do not sell your personal information or health information, as those terms are defined under applicable law, to any third party. We do not share your data with data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. Your data is used solely to provide you the Base service.

4. How Your Data Is Stored

We use Supabase as our data processor to host our database, authentication, and backend infrastructure. Supabase stores your data on our behalf, encrypted at rest and in transit, and does not use it for its own purposes. Supabase acts strictly as a processor under our instructions. It is not an independent data controller of your information.

Access to production data is restricted to what's operationally necessary to run the service (e.g. responding to a support request or investigating a security incident).

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as long as needed to provide the service to you as a guest user on your device. If you delete your account, your synced data is permanently deleted from our servers as part of that process (see Section 6). Data that exists only locally on your device is controlled entirely by you and removed when you delete the app.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account permanently and irreversibly deletes your cloud-synced data and your account. Data still stored locally on your device is not affected by this and is controlled by you directly (e.g. by deleting the app).
  • Sign out: Signing out removes the cloud connection on that device without deleting your account or synced data.
  • Access or export your data: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you by contacting us at the email below.
  • Correct your data: Any log or profile field can be edited or deleted directly in the app.

7. Washington My Health My Data Act: Consumer Health Data Notice

This section is provided specifically to comply with the Washington My Health My Data Act ("MHMDA"), which applies to information that could reasonably be linked to a consumer and relates to their physical or mental health. This applies to Base regardless of where you live, as a matter of policy consistency.

Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

  • Symptom and severity data (pain, fatigue, brain fog, mood, stress levels)
  • Sleep data
  • Medication names, dosages, and adherence logs
  • Self-reported chronic condition/diagnosis categories
  • Data that could reveal an attempt to seek health care services (e.g. generating a doctor report)

Purpose for Collection

We collect this data solely to provide the core Base service to you: displaying your own logs back to you, computing pattern insights, generating PDF reports at your request, and (if you opt in) syncing that data across your devices.

Our Practices

  • We do not sell consumer health data.
  • We do not share consumer health data with third parties for any purpose outside providing you the service, except as necessary to use our processor, Supabase, strictly under our instructions.
  • We do not use geofences around health care facilities to collect or infer health data.

Your MHMDA Rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we are collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data;
  • Access your consumer health data;
  • Withdraw consent for our collection or sharing of your consumer health data, and have it deleted (in-app account deletion fulfills this directly);
  • Appeal a denial of a rights request.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond consistent with the timelines required under the MHMDA.

8. Children's Privacy

Base is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes we'll provide notice within the app.
