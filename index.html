Why I built this

I built Base after watching how hard it is to be taken seriously by doctors when you're managing a chronic condition that doesn't show up on a scan. Logging should take seconds, not become its own burden — and your data should actually help you get believed, not just sit in an app nobody reads.

How it works

1

Log in seconds

A few taps a day — pain, fatigue, sleep, mood, and whatever else might be a trigger.

2

Spot real patterns

Base finds statistically backed connections in your own data, not guesses.

3

Walk in prepared

Generate a clean report for your doctor covering your flares, meds, and patterns.

Base shows patterns in your logged data — it's informational, not a diagnosis. It is not a medical device and does not treat or provide medical advice. Always consult a doctor before making medical decisions.

FAQ

Is my data private?

Yes. Your symptom data is never sold or shared for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

When does Base launch?

We're finishing up development now — join the waitlist above and you'll be the first to know when it's live.

Will logging always be free?

Yes. Daily symptom logging, history, and streaks are free forever. Only advanced pattern insights and doctor reports are part of the paid Pro tier.

What conditions is this for?

Base is built for people managing fibromyalgia, lupus, EDS, MCAS, POTS, and other chronic pain and autoimmune conditions.
